How It All Began

The Story of Unknown Coaching Centre

Every great journey begins with a single step. Ours began with just 3 students and an unshakeable belief that quality education can change lives.

🌱
8 December 2023

The Beginning — Teen Bacche, Ek Sapna

When Himanshu Tiwari decided to start a coaching centre in Rewa, he had no fancy classroom, no big budget — just a passion to teach and a belief in every student's potential. On 8th December 2023, with just 3 students, Unknown Coaching Centre took its first breath.

2024 — Growth

Word Spread, Family Grew

One by one, students started joining. Parents trusted Mannu Sir. Word of mouth did what no advertisement could. The "unknown" centre was slowly becoming known — not just for teaching, but for genuinely caring about every student.
